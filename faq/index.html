Documents

Indian nationals need: (1) Valid Indian driving licence — LMV (Light Motor Vehicle) category. (2) Government-issued photo ID — Aadhaar card, Passport, or Voter ID. Foreign nationals need: (1) International Driving Permit (IDP). (2) Original passport. Both are needed at the time of vehicle handover.

Policies

Inter-state travel requires prior written permission from us and may require a state permit. Day trips to Hampi, Dandeli, or Mangalore may be possible with advance notice. There may be an additional charge for inter-state use. Please always inform us before planning any cross-state trips.
Our 24/7 support team is one call away. In case of a breakdown, call us immediately and we'll dispatch roadside assistance as quickly as possible. For mechanical failures that are not the result of driver negligence, we arrange a replacement vehicle at no extra cost.
In the event of an accident: (1) Ensure everyone's safety. (2) Call the police for an FIR (First Information Report). (3) Immediately notify our team. The comprehensive insurance covers third-party damage. Own damage from accidents may involve a deductible depending on circumstances — details are explained in your rental agreement.
Smoking is strictly not permitted in any of our vehicles. A cleaning fee of ₹2,000 will be charged if the vehicle is returned with evidence of smoking inside.
Pets are allowed in most vehicles with prior notice. We ask that you use a pet carrier or seat cover to protect the upholstery. An additional cleaning deposit of ₹1,000 may be collected. Please inform us when booking.

International Drivers

Yes! Foreign nationals can rent self-drive cars in Goa with a valid International Driving Permit (IDP) issued in their home country, plus their original passport. We regularly serve tourists from Europe, the UK, Australia, the US, and other countries — the process is straightforward.
Driving licences from countries that have signed the 1949 Geneva Convention on Road Traffic are valid in India for 30 days from arrival. After 30 days, an IDP is required. Countries include the UK, USA, Australia, most EU nations, and many others. When in doubt, obtain an IDP before travel — it's simple to get in your home country.
Goa is one of India's most foreigner-friendly driving destinations. Roads are generally good, signage is in English, and traffic is lighter than most Indian cities. We recommend starting with a smaller car (Swift or i20) if you're not used to Indian traffic, and always driving on the left. Our team shares a local road safety guide with every international renter.
