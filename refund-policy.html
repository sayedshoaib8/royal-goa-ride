Refund and Cancellation Policy

The following summary reflects Royal Goa Ride's published rental cancellation terms. Review the full rental policies for booking, deposit and vehicle-return details.

Policy summary

  1. Advance refunds
  2. Travel emergencies
  3. Request a refund
  4. Security deposits

Refunds for booking advances

Refund amounts depend on how long before the scheduled pickup time the cancellation is made:

  • 48 hours or more before pickup: 100% of the advance paid is refunded.
  • 24 to 48 hours before pickup: 50% of the advance paid is refunded; the remaining 50% is retained to cover vehicle reservation costs.
  • Less than 24 hours before pickup: the advance is non-refundable.

Cyclone, extreme flooding or declared emergency

If a cyclone, extreme flood event or government-declared emergency in Goa makes travel impossible, the published policy provides for rescheduling or a full refund regardless of the cancellation window.

How to cancel and request a refund

Send a WhatsApp message with your booking reference and cancellation reason to +91 99753 56697. The published rental policy says refunds are processed within 3–5 business days via the original payment method.

Security deposits

A security deposit is separate from an advance payment for a booking. The published rental terms describe deposit deductions for matters such as damage beyond normal wear, missing accessories, traffic challans incurred during the rental, or a fuel shortfall. Any deduction is itemised. See the full rental policies for the applicable deposit and cancellation details.

For questions about an existing booking, contact Royal Goa Ride with your booking details before the scheduled pickup time.
