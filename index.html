Common Questions

We've Got Answers

You need a valid Indian driving licence (LMV category) and a government-issued photo ID such as Aadhaar or Passport. Foreign nationals need an International Driving Permit (IDP) along with their passport. We accept all valid IDPs — no extra paperwork.
The rental policy includes delivery to hotels, airports, and railway stations across Goa during delivery hours. Share your location and arrival time so we can confirm the arrangements.
The deposit is refundable subject to the rental policy. Deductions may apply for damage beyond normal wear, missing accessories, traffic challans, or fuel shortfalls. Refund timing varies by vehicle; see the rental policy for details.
Review the rental policy for the applicable rate, deposit, delivery terms, and possible charges. These can include fuel shortfalls, damage beyond normal wear, missing accessories, traffic challans, after-hours delivery surcharges, or approved out-of-state permit fees.
Inter-state travel requires prior permission and appropriate permits. Popular day trips like Dandeli or Hampi may be possible with advance notice. Please confirm with our team before planning any cross-state trips — we'll guide you on what's needed.
