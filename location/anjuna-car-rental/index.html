Explore Anjuna: Goa's Alternative Beach

Anjuna isn't about luxury. It's about authenticity. Hippies settled here in the 1960s and never left. The culture persisted. Every Wednesday, vendors transform the beach into a flea market: vintage clothes, handmade jewelry, artwork, street food. Backpackers mix with bohemian artists. The rocky shoreline creates natural pools perfect for swimming.

Your car gives you the freedom to explore Anjuna's hidden corners: coves accessible only by car, sunset points overlooking the Arabian Sea, small cafes tucked into hills. Wednesday flea market is the main draw, but Anjuna is worth visiting every day for its authentic Goa spirit.

Wednesday Flea Market

9 AM–5 PM. Fashion, crafts, souvenirs, food. Free entry. Thousands of vendors & tourists. Your car = parking stress-free.

Rocky Beaches

Natural rock formations. Rock pools for swimming. Sunset spots with views. Less commercial than Calangute. Peaceful mornings.

Bohemian Cafes

Quirky bookstalls, live music venues, spiritual spaces. Beers ₹150–300. Mains ₹300–700. Authentic Goa culture preserved.

Trance Parties

Underground electronic music scene. Off-circuit warehouse parties (check social media). Your car essential for discovery.

Must-Do: Wednesday Flea Market Guide

Anjuna's Wednesday flea market is legendary. Here's how to make the most of it with your rental car.

Arrive Early

7–8 AM: Beat crowds. Parking is abundant. Browse without crush. By 11 AM, thousands arrive. By 2 PM, shoulder-to-shoulder.

Parking Strategy

Free parking on residential roads, 5-min walk from flea market. Avoid main beach parking (congested by noon). Your car = flexibility to park anywhere & explore at your pace.

Shopping Categories

Clothing: Vintage, bohemian wear (₹300–1,500). Jewelry: Silver, beads (₹500–3,000). Art: Paintings, sculptures (₹1,000–10,000+). Food: Street snacks, juice stalls (₹100–300).

Bargaining Tips

Bargain is expected. Start at 50% of asking price. Meet at 60–70%. Sellers are reasonable. Bundle purchases for discounts. Avoid arguing — vibe kills sales.

Beach After Market

Spend afternoon at beach. Swim, relax, eat at shacks. Watch sunset from rock viewpoints (6 PM). Flea market ends by 5 PM. Traffic clears by 6 PM.

Post-Market Dining

Stay for dinner at local cafe. Relax with beers, wine, food. Music often plays. Authentic traveler vibe. Return to hotel after sunset.
