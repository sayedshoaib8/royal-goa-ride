Airport Car Rental FAQs

What time can I book a car for airport pickup?
+

We operate 24/7 for airport pickups. Book on WhatsApp anytime, and we'll have your car ready within 30 minutes of your flight landing. Let us know your flight number, and we'll track your landing time.

Do I need to pay extra for airport delivery?
+

No. Our published daily rates include free delivery to Dabolim Airport. You pay only the rental rate (from ₹1,200/day) plus a refundable security deposit (₹5,000–₹15,000 depending on vehicle).

What documents do I need to rent a car?
+

Minimum: Valid driver's license (national or international), passport/ID proof, one photo. If paying by card, we'll hold the security deposit (auto-released after return). If cash, deposit is refundable upon vehicle return.

Can I drop off at a different location (e.g., my hotel)?
+

Yes. We offer one-way rentals. Pickup at Dabolim Airport, drop-off anywhere in Goa. No extra charge. Just let us know the drop-off location when booking.

Is there a minimum rental period?
+

Minimum is 24 hours. Longer rentals (3+ days) qualify for discounts: 10% off at 3 days, 20% off at 7+ days. Ask us for details when you book.

What's included in the rental?
+

Full tank of fuel, basic insurance, GPS, free roadside support 24/7, unlimited kilometers. Fuel top-ups on longer trips are on you. AC is always on. Parking is your responsibility.

What if I have an accident or breakdown?
+

Call us immediately at +91 99753 56697. We have 24/7 roadside assistance. Minor breakdowns are on us. Major accidents: you pay excess per policy (₹5,000–₹10,000). Always report within 2 hours.
