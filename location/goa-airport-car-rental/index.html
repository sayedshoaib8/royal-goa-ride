Self-Drive Car Rental at Goa's Airports

Goa is served by Dabolim Airport (GOI) and Manohar International Airport at Mopa (GOX). Airport delivery can be arranged across Goa; share your airport and arrival time so we can confirm availability and coordinate the handover.

Airport Delivery

Share your arrival airport and flight details when requesting a booking. We will confirm vehicle availability and the handover arrangements.

Delivery Timing

Standard delivery hours are 7:00 AM–10:00 PM. Earlier or later handovers may be arranged with advance notice and may attract a surcharge; confirm timing when booking.

Insurance & Fuel Policy

Vehicles carry mandatory third-party insurance, and own-damage insurance is included in the rental rate. The fuel policy is full-to-full. Review the rental policy and contact us if you need help during your rental.

Pickup and Return

Share your preferred pickup and return locations when booking. We will confirm the arrangements and any applicable terms before confirming your rental.

From Dabolim Airport, You Can Reach:

Goa's top beaches, heritage sites, and attractions are 30–90 minutes away by car. Having your own vehicle means you skip crowds and set your own pace.

North Goa (45 min)

Calangute, Baga, Anjuna beaches. Party vibes, water sports, shacks. Best for young travelers and families.

South Goa (60 min)

Palolem, Benaulim, Colva beaches. Quieter, more upscale. Backwater cruises, coconut plantations.

Panjim City Center (30 min)

Heritage, cafes, riverside walks. Portuguese architecture, bookstalls, Sunday flea markets.

Dudhsagar Falls (90 min)

400m waterfall, trek-worthy. Best in monsoon. Mahindra Thar or Innova recommended.

Airport Car Rental FAQs

What time can I book a car for airport pickup?
+

Send your airport, arrival date and time, and flight details by WhatsApp. We will confirm vehicle availability and coordinate the handover; booking confirmations are sent within 30 minutes during business hours.

Do I need to pay extra for airport delivery?
+

Delivery to airports across Goa is included under the rental policy. Standard delivery hours are 7:00 AM–10:00 PM; an early or late handover may attract a surcharge. Confirm timing and any applicable terms when booking.

What documents do I need to rent a car?
+

Bring the original documents listed in the rental policy, including a valid driving licence and accepted ID. Foreign nationals should check the additional document requirements before booking.

Can I drop off at a different location (e.g., my hotel)?
+

Tell us your preferred pickup and return locations when you request a booking. We will confirm whether the arrangements are available and explain any applicable terms before confirming.

Is there a minimum rental period?
+

Rental rates depend on the vehicle and dates. Contact us for the current quote and rental terms for your trip.

What's included in the rental?
+

Vehicles carry mandatory third-party insurance, and own-damage insurance is included in the rental rate. The fuel policy is full-to-full. See the rental policy for the terms.

What if I have an accident or breakdown?
+

Prioritize everyone's safety and contact emergency services where needed. Then contact us using the emergency number in the rental policy. Insurance, damage, and recovery terms are governed by the rental policy and rental agreement.
